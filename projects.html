Digital design

Asynchronous FIFO Buffer w/ Scoreboard Verification

Parameterized dual-clock FIFO in SystemVerilog that safely passes data between two independent clock domains using Gray-coded pointers and two-flop synchronizers. Verified with a self-checking scoreboard testbench running independent read/write clocks, plus directed corner-case tests for full, empty, and pointer wrap-around.

SystemVerilog Verilog Vivado
